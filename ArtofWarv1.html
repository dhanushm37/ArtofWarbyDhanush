📜 Codex & Commander's Guide

🏰 How to Play & Shortcuts

• Placement: Select your target side, troop type, and level, then click any grid slot to deploy.

• Merging Mechanic: Placing a troop on top of an identical troop of the exact same level combines them into the next level (up to Level 10 max).

• Row-Fill Shortcut (`⚡`): Click the lightning button next to any row to instantly fill the entire 9 slots with your currently selected troop type and level.

🪄 Pharaoh Special Ability

When the Pharaoh drops to 80% low on health (≤20% HP), he triggers a divine healing buff: regenerates 5% of his max health every 3 seconds for 15 seconds (5 ticks total, once per battle).
